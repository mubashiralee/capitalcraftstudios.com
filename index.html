About us

We design it. We build it. Then we run it.

CapitalCraft Studios is a design and engineering studio in Reading, England. We build apps, games, websites and business systems for companies, and we stay after launch: releases, live events, support and the servers behind them.

We prove everything on our own products first. Money Tycoon is live on the App Store and Google Play in eight languages, and every release passes the audits, tests and save checks we now bring to client work.

Next, we are taking the same craft to design tools for art and fashion.

Principles

Six rules. No exceptions.

Each one has a paper trail.

  • Nothing ships unaudited.

    Money Tycoon has more than 600 audit reports on file.

  • Old saves always load.

    Money Tycoon’s save format is on version 108.

  • Every fix leaves a test behind.

    More than 30,000 automated tests guard Money Tycoon.

  • Fair play, in writing.

    Fair Play Manifesto, version 3, 18 May 2026.

  • Every language ships on day one.

    Eight languages. No screen goes out in English only.

  • A bug found is a bug owned.

    House rule since 26 April 2026: “not ours” is not an answer.
